# BRIEF: Truly Tours Namibia

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe**: Warm, grounded, unhurried, photographic, honest. References: a
   well-printed field guide, a national park trailhead board, the light in a
   Sossusvlei photograph at 7am.
2. **Scroll journey, section by section**: The visitor lands on the dunes at
   Sossusvlei with the first tour already in view. Then the whole route of the
   country draws itself as they scroll. Then the full set of tours passes
   sideways, one card per tour. Then the day-by-day routes fold open under
   accordions, the experiences list, what past guests said, the photo wall,
   and the last screen is the operator's own contact plate.
3. **Energy curve**: Calm open, a quiet middle where the route draws, a long
   broad middle where the set travels sideways, then practical, then warm, then
   resolved and still.
4. **Feeling stage by stage, and the ONE moment**: arrival awe, curiosity as the
   route draws, breadth as the set passes, appetite at the experiences, trust at
   the guest words, resolve at the contact plate. The one moment they remember:
   "the map of Namibia drew itself along the bottom while I walked the tour
   collection". That moment is the peak's companion; the peak itself is the
   sideways walk through all six tours.
5. **One thing no site they have seen does**: The route rail: a real line
   through the country's names, drawn by scroll position, stamping a waypoint
   for each section passed, and clickable to jump. It is the navigation, the
   progress bar and the souvenir at the end, in one line.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal**: Editorial-gallery. Paper ground, warm
   ink, one burnt-orange accent, museum labels, generous air. Not brutalist,
   not maximalist, not dark-lux.
7. **One unbroken world or distinct scenes?** Distinct scenes, hard grounds per
   room. The page is a walkable collection, not one camera flight.
8. **Assets they already have**: Real photography: the client's own trip photos
   (WhatsApp set) plus sourced destination frames (Sossusvlei, Fish River
   Canyon, Kolmanskop, aerial dunes, zebra, rhino, elephant and more, all
   optimized WebP already in /public/images). The client's raster wordmark.
   No footage. No generation, no spend: the photographic world is the brand.

## What this is, and who it is for

Truly Tours Namibia runs guided, small-group Namibia tours from Windhoek: 3 to
13 day loops to Sossusvlei, Etosha, Swakopmund and the Kalahari, plus city,
township and cultural walks. It is for travellers planning a first or second
Namibia trip who want a local operator with a real guide, park fees and
accommodation handled.

## What the visitor must believe by the end

A Windhoek team that grew up guiding will run the whole trip properly, for a
price confirmed in writing, and one WhatsApp message starts it.

## What the visitor does next

"WhatsApp us" (mailto and tel offered beside it). One label, used everywhere.

## The journey beats

```
1  Recognition   the dunes, already on screen, the first tour labelled
2  Orientation   the country's route draws itself under the visitor's hand
3  Range         all six tours pass sideways, each with the same label schema
4  Substance     the day-by-day routes open under accordions
5  Appetite      the experiences beyond the big loops, and the guest words
6  Commitment    the contact plate, typeset like the labels above it
```

## Feeling curve (one line per act: emotion, then what causes it)

```
1  Awe         the Sossusvlei scene as layered planes, the label arriving
2  Curiosity   the route line drawing through the country's names, quiet
3  Breadth     the tours rail travelling sideways, one label at a time  (PEAK)
4  Appetite    the experiences opening under accordions, facts not pitch
5  Trust       the guest words, one at a time, in a slider
6  Resolve     the contact plate, still, holding
```

## The peak

The tours rail: the largest span on the page, the widest travel, the act the
route line stamps its biggest waypoint for. Visitor's sentence: "I walked past
every tour they run, sideways, and each one told me its days, its route and
what is included."

## Tell-someone sentence

"It's the site where a map line of Namibia draws itself along the bottom of the
page as you walk the tour collection, and every tour is a museum label, not a
pitch."

## Authored silence

The act before the peak (the route drawing) is deliberately quiet: one thin
line and place names, nothing else moving. That silence is the peak's run-up.
