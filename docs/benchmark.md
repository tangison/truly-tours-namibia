# Niche benchmark: guided Namibia tours from Windhoek

Researched 2026-11-23 (live web search, results in the build transcript).

## Top digital players in the niche

1. **Chameleon Safaris** — chameleonsafaris.com. Windhoek-based operator running
   scheduled small-group camping and lodge safaris with expert local guides.
   The closest direct competitor to Truly Tours Namibia: same origin city, same
   tour format, strongest direct competitor website in the niche.
2. **TourRadar's Namibia vertical** — tourradar.com/d/namibia. Marketplace with
   1,000+ reviewed Namibia tours. Benchmark for conversion patterns (review
   counts, day-by-day clarity, price anchoring) rather than brand design.
3. **SafariBookings' Namibia section** — safaribookings.com/tours/namibia.
   Largest safari marketplace listing (162 Namibia tours, 27 operators).
   Benchmark for structured tour data presentation.

## Selected baseline target

**Chameleon Safaris (chameleonsafaris.com)** is the baseline: the undisputed
direct competitor for "guided tours from Windhoek", reachable, and with the
most polished independent operator site in the exact niche.

## Side-by-side craft audit (this build vs baseline)

| Dimension | Baseline bar | This build |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Layout density | Clear rows, day-by-day itineraries | Tour rows with full day-by-day itineraries, equal-edge 6x2 photo wall |
| Typographic discipline | One grotesk family, tight scale | Schibsted Grotesk throughout, tracked display scale |
| Spatial pacing | Sectioned scroll | One-deal rhythm: surface pairs, pinned chapters moment, photo wall |
| Interaction polish | Hover states, simple galleries | GSAP scrubbed chapters, developing-photo moment, zoom wall |
| Differentiation | Professional but template-like | Editorial "dealt cards" look derived from the client's own logo palette and photography |

Quality matches or exceeds the baseline on typography, pacing and interaction
without cloning Chameleon's proprietary brand identity.
